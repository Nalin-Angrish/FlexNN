Detailed Description

Implements FlexNN::Activations helpers (to_string, try_parse, dispatch).

Why out-of-line: to_string and try_parse are non-trivial (string handling) and not hot — keeping them in a .cpp avoids inlining bloat and keeps Activation.hpp small for the many layer headers that include it. The per-activation math stays header-only inline (see include/activations) so the compiler can vectorize it at call sites.
